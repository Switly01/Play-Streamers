# Play Streamers

Play Streamers is a Windows creator workspace that brings stream analytics, content planning, community routines, brand tools and revenue insights into one focused desktop app.

## Highlights

- Server-collected stream history with average and peak viewer summaries
- Verified channel events from Kick and Play Connect
- Content ideas, stream schedules and creator workspaces
- Community routines, brand tools and media-kit preparation
- A consistent workspace across Free, Pro and Product Pro
- SW Bot diagnostics with clear SW AI explanations
- Secure account and plan synchronization through SW Identity
- Session protection through Windows Credential Manager

The app is free to download and its core workspace remains available through Play Streamers Free. Play Streamers Pro and Product Pro are optional paid subscriptions managed outside the Microsoft Store and unlock additional tools. Locked features are clearly labeled in the app. This is not a time-limited trial; customers can continue using Free without purchasing.

Play Streamers 0.14.1 does not stream, record, capture camera or microphone input, provide a virtual camera, or include Studio. It does not collect or upload raw media. Live channel events and stream history are displayed only from verified data associated with the connected account.

## What's new

Version 0.14.1 removes the obsolete Studio/local-engine connection, improves the SW Identity desktop handoff, retains Windows 10 compatibility and aligns the Store listing with the app's actual feature set.

A focused creator workspace for server analytics, content, community and brand tools. Free remains usable; Pro plans are optional.
